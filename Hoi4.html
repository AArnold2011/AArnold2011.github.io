Hearts of Iron 4

Hearts of Iron is a game where you play as a country durring the lead up to WWII

In this game, you can choose to play as any country in the world and make decisions that affect the outcome of the war.

You can also manage your country's economy, research technology, and build up your military to fight the war.

Overall, Hearts of Iron is known for its high level of strategic depth and engaging gameplay, making it a popular choice among fans of military simulation games.
