Welding

Welding is a fabrication process that joins materials, usually metals or thermoplastics, by causing coalescence. This is often done by heating the materials to a suitable temperature for the material to melt and then cooling it to form a joint. Welding is used in many industries, including construction, automotive manufacturing, and aerospace engineering.

There are several types of welding processes, including arc welding, gas tungsten arc welding (GTAW), gas metal arc welding (GMAW), and resistance welding. Each method has its own advantages and applications depending on the materials being joined and the required strength of the joint.

Welding requires proper safety equipment such as protective clothing, helmets with filters, gloves, and ventilation systems to protect against harmful fumes and radiation. It also requires skill and practice to produce high-quality welds that meet industry standards.

Overall, welding is an essential skill in many trades and industries where strong joints are needed to hold structures together or assemble components into larger assemblies.
