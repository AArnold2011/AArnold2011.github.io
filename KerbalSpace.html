Kerbal Space Program

Kerbal Space Program is a space flight simulation game developed and published by Squad. The game allows players to design, build, and launch spacecraft in a fictional universe. Players can explore the solar system, establish colonies on other planets, and conduct scientific experiments. The game features a realistic physics engine that simulates orbital mechanics, atmospheric entry, and spacecraft behavior. Players can choose from a variety of rockets, probes, and space stations to build and launch into space. Kerbal Space Program has a large and active player base, with regular updates and expansions that introduce new vehicles, maps, and gameplay features. The game also includes a single-player mode with missions and campaigns for players who prefer to play solo. Overall, Kerbal Space Program is known for its detailed spacecraft models, realistic physics, and engaging gameplay, making it a popular choice among fans of space simulation games.
